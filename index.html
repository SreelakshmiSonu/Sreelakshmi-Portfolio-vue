About Me

I'm a Frontend Developer

I have 5+ years of experience building fast, scalable, and accessible web applications. I specialise in React, Tailwind, and GraphQL, crafting clean UI architectures and delivering production‑ready interfaces that feel smooth, intuitive, and reliable.

I build modern, accessible user interfaces with Vue 3 and the Composition API, using TypeScript to create clear, maintainable components. I enjoy turning designs into polished experiences and shaping reusable UI that works consistently across an application.

My frontend experience also includes React and Angular, along with tools such as GraphQL and Tailwind CSS. This breadth helps me adapt to different products and teams while keeping usability, performance and code quality at the centre of my work.

I enjoy working closely with designers and engineers in Agile teams to solve real product problems. From integrating APIs to refining responsive details, I focus on delivering dependable interfaces that are intuitive for users and straightforward for teams to maintain.
