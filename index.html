About Me

I'm a Frontend Developer

I have 5+ years of experience building fast, scalable, and accessible web applications. I specialise in React, Tailwind, and GraphQL, crafting clean UI architectures and delivering production‑ready interfaces that feel smooth, intuitive, and reliable.

My work centres around the modern Angular ecosystem—reactive programming with RxJS, Signals, clean architecture, interceptors, and scalable component design. I focus on building fast, accessible, and visually polished interfaces using Angular, TypeScript, T ailwind CSS, and Angular Material.

I specialise in transforming Figma designs into production‑ready Angular experiences, creating reusable component systems, shared libraries, and UI patterns that scale across large teams and enterprise applications.

Alongside Angular, I also work with React, Vue 3, and the Composition API, giving me a broad perspective across modern frontend frameworks and helping me choose the right patterns for each project.

I thrive in Agile environments, enjoy collaborating closely with designers and engineers, and love mentoring teammates in Angular best practices. Whether it’s performance optimisation, UI system design, or integrating complex APIs, I focus on delivering solutions that are elegant, maintainable, and built for long‑term scalability.
